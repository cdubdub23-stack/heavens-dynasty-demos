The Man
From Detroit to the World Stage

Born November 20, 1984 on the east side of Detroit, Michigan. At Warren Fitzgerald High School, Carlos became a 4-year varsity starter ranked the 6th best point guard in Michigan — AP Class B All-State. They told him he was too small at 5'9". He told them to watch.

"They told me I was too small. I told them to watch me work."

From Mott Community College in Flint to Cleveland State University (career-high 18 pts vs Wright State, 12 assists vs Michigan State) to the NBA D-League (Dakota Wizards, Fort Wayne Mad Ants) — every step was a grind. Then came the call: the Harlem Globetrotters. For 11 years as "Dizzy" #2, he traveled the world entertaining millions with his legendary ball spins, slide dribbles, and four-point shots.

Now in Nashville, Carlos coaches at Christ Presbyterian Academy, runs Carlos English Elite AAU (#36 nationally by Future150), and pours everything into the next generation. Father. Husband. Speaker. Legend.
