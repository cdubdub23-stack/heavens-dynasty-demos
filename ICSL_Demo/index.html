Terry Pettis built ICSL.
He's been building it for 15+ years.

Born North Minneapolis, 1984. Patrick Henry High School class of 2002. Three-time Minnesota Class AAA state basketball champion. Played two seasons at Fresno State University.

Convicted of first-degree murder in July 2006 at age 21. Sentenced to Life Without Parole. In the 21 years since: AA/NA, Lifer's Group, Peer Support Specialist, currently in school. Founded and runs ICSL across multiple sports.

If it works at the toughest yard in the state,
it works at every other yard in California.

CSP-SAC is a Level 4 institution. If ICSL's structure holds at the highest-security population in the state, it holds at lower-security facilities by definition. CDCR has 33 institutions. ICSL exists at one. The cost of expansion is administrative buy-in + equipment + uniforms.

ICSL is ready to scale.

"ICSL is needed statewide if they don't have a sports community."— Christopher Costello, current ICSL player at CSP-SAC

The architect cannot be replaced by a memo.

ICSL is not a curriculum. It is a leadership culture Terry Pettis personally embodies. Statewide expansion needs a founder who can train operators at each facility, hold the standard, and stay credible to incarcerated populations and corrections leadership at once.

Commutation would unlock the only person positioned to scale ICSL across California.
