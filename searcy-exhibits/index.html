Detroit Police Department Laboratory Analysis report, Lab No. LDS-60149, Incident No. 04-108259. Trial Transcript, Page 119 — Prosecutor Muscat's False Statement on the Record
Trial transcript, page 119, lines 13-17. Deputy Chief Prosecutor Patrick Muscat (P51482) states on the record: "In regards to discovery, there have been some off-the-record conversations about an in-store video. This statement was materially false, as the VHS tape had already been collected, processed, and used to generate still images and DVDs by Detroit Police personnel.
